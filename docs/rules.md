# Nuzlocke Rules

### Pokemon Catching:
- Only the first wild pokemon encountered in each area may be caught. If it faints or flees you're out of luck until the next area!
	- Goes into effect AFTER you've gotten pokeballs. (Pokemon encountered before you can catch them dont count)
	- **(OPTIONAL) Dupes clause:** If you already have a pokemon that is in the same evolutionary line as the first pokemon encountered in an area, you can catch the next encounter that you don't have already.
	- **HM Helper:** If the player has no Pokémon that can use a field move that is required to continue the game, they may catch another Pokémon to use the required field move. However, this Pokémon cannot be used in battle and must be released as soon as it is no longer needed or if the player catches another Pokémon that can use the same field move.
- If you encounter a shiny pokemon it may be caught whether or not you've already caught a pokemon in that area! 
- Gift pokemon (ie Lapras, Eevee (or in this case the pokemon that will randomly replace them)) can be used and are not restricted. 
- Nicknames are mandatory, you have to nickname every pokemon!

### Pokemon DEATH
- Any Pokémon that faints is considered dead and must be released OR permanently boxed. No revives allowed.
- In the event of a blackout/whiteout, you may continue your run with:
	1. One "surviving" pokemon from your party, and any pokemon you have in boxes ("non-deceased").
	2. Only your pokemon saved in boxes ("non-deceased").

### Other Exceptions
- **HM Helper:** If the player has no Pokémon that can use a field move that is required to continue the game, they may catch another Pokémon to use the required field move. However, this Pokémon cannot be used in battle and must be released as soon as it is no longer needed or if the player catches another Pokémon that can use the same field move.
